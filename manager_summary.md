# SokoniHub manager summary
This system provides indicators of potential listing mismatch and does not replace human review.

## Risks
Manual labels can be wrong, and local words can be absent from the vocabulary. The three synthetic examples cannot establish performance on real seller photos. Brand claims cannot be verified from these scores. Real uploads may reveal private information.

## Mitigations
Ask a person to review flagged listings and invite seller clarification. Keep the original title intact. Check photo permissions and remove unnecessary identifying details before a pilot. Keep generated illustrations separate and clearly labeled. Evaluate a larger, independently labeled and multilingual sample before setting an operational threshold.

## What We Will Not Claim
We do not claim verified authenticity, automatic fraud detection, perfect accuracy or guaranteed correctness. This demo compares manually supplied image labels with listing words; it does not understand image pixels. Costs here are local CPU work, with no paid model calls; production review and hosting would still cost money.

AfyaPlus posters may be a future transfer use case, but AfyaPlus clinical fixtures were intentionally not mixed into this SokoniHub catalog.
