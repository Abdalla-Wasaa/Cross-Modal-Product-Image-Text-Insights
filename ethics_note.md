# Photos
The included SVGs are synthetic teaching fixtures, not seller photos. Manual tags are supplied by the fixture author. No face detector runs. Before accepting real seller uploads, a reviewer must check for faces, children, addresses and phone numbers, obtain appropriate permission, and crop or blur unnecessary identifying details. Restrict original-photo access and agree on deletion periods before any production pilot.

# Language
ASCII English tokens miss Swahili terms, spelling variants and local product names. A low score can reflect vocabulary gaps rather than seller error. Reviewers should ask the seller to clarify in their preferred language and record corrections to annotations separately from titles. Audio sidecars are synthetic; real speech needs consent, restricted access and a deletion policy.

# Generated Pixels
The separate generation directory contains a labeled SVG stub concept. It is not a photo of the SKU and is excluded from scoring and gold checks. Never substitute generated concepts for seller evidence. Record generator version, prompt hash and output provenance for any future generator.

# Review Path
A flag enters a manual review queue; it does not remove a listing or penalize a seller. A reviewer compares the original image, tags, title and description, checks language gaps, and requests seller clarification where needed. Record reviewer, reason, evidence and decision in an access-controlled audit record. Sellers must be able to dispute a decision. Publishing, title changes and sanctions require a human decision. This prototype describes that process; it does not implement a queue or audit service.
